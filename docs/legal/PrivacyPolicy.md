# MilanSetu Privacy Policy — Version 2026-09-18

MilanSetu processes information necessary to provide accounts, profiles, matching, communication, safety, verification and security features.

## Information users provide

This may include account information, profile information, photographs, preferences, verification information and information users choose to share through platform features.

## Security information

For account security, MilanSetu may maintain session and security audit information such as login time, IP address, application/device metadata, browser information and security events.

MilanSetu does not intentionally collect IMEI, MAC address, SIM identifiers or operating-system usernames for ordinary login security auditing.

## Legal acceptance records

MilanSetu records the legal document type, version and acceptance timestamp. Where appropriate for security and audit purposes, the record may also contain the server-observed IP address and user-agent.

## User responsibility

Users remain responsible for information they voluntarily provide and for independently verifying material information about other users before making significant decisions.

This policy should be reviewed and updated for the final production deployment with advice appropriate to the jurisdictions and services in which MilanSetu operates.
